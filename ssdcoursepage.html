About

Software System Development is a graduate-level course that equips students with a comprehensive understanding of the principles, tools, and practices involved in designing and building modern software systems.

The course explores topics including Operating Systems, Computer Networks, Software Engineering methodologies, and Database Systems including relational and NoSQL databases.

Students gain practical experience with Bash scripting, SQL, NoSQL databases, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, front-end frameworks, RESTful APIs, Python, and Flask.

The course focuses on building scalable, maintainable and secure software systems while developing an understanding of system integration, version control, development workflows and deployment strategies.

Course Policy

Teaching and Learning Methodology

The course is designed to help learners understand the principles behind good software system development through practical, real-life problems.

Academic Honesty

Students are expected to complete their own work and develop the skills required for software development.

Zero Tolerance on Plagiarism

Any form of cheating or plagiarism can result in a score of zero for projects, assignments or exams. Serious cases may result in an F grade.

Absentee Policy

Students are expected to attend and participate in lectures and lab sessions. Missed quizzes or activities will not have makeup sessions.

Miscellaneous Instructions

Students should have a laptop with a decent configuration and participate actively in lectures, discussions and labs.

Use of AI Tools

Students may use AI tools such as ChatGPT and Copilot for course assignments and activities. Students should mention what tools were used and how they were used in the submission report.
